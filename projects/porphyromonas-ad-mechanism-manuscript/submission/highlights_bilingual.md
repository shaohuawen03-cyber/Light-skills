# Highlights / 亮点

## English

- An aggregate oral-smORF workflow was reconstructed with explicit denominators.
- Peptide counts were not treated as independent healthy–periodontitis replicates.
- NTxPred2 evaluated 3,299 of 3,518 BBB-high periodontitis-branch candidates.
- The source reported a 12-candidate main set and an 8-candidate stricter set.
- Missing sequences and row-level scores prevent candidate reproduction or validation.

- 未把肽计数视为健康—牙周炎比较的独立生物学重复。
- NTxPred2 评价了 3,518 条 BBB 高分牙周炎分支候选中的 3,299 条。
