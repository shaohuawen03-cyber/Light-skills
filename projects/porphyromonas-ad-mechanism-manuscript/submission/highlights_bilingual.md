# Highlights / 亮点

## English

- An aggregate oral-smORF funnel was reconstructed with explicit denominators.
- Peptide counts were not treated as independent cohort-level replicates.
- Twelve external-v0.4 candidate sequences were compositionally audited.
- Source-reported AChE docking means ranged from −9.60 to −8.25 kcal/mol.
- Missing screening lineage, raw docking artefacts and experiments limit mechanism claims.
