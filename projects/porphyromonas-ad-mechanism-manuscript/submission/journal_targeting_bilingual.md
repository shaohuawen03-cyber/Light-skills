# Journal targeting / 期刊定位

Checked: 2026-08-12. Indexing and scope can change; authors must recheck the official journal site and Clarivate Master Journal List immediately before submission.

## Recommendation / 建议

### Primary target for author discussion: BMC Oral Health

**Why it is in scope:** The official journal page states that it covers disorders of the mouth, teeth and gums, including molecular genetics, pathophysiology, epidemiology, and oral microbiology. It also states current Web of Science SCIE indexing.

**Why acceptance risk remains high:** The package now contains twelve externally reported sequence strings and a source-reported AChE docking summary, but it lacks their principal-source row-level lineage, sample mappings, predictor rows, original screening code, raw docking artefacts, independent validation, and a valid participant-level comparison. A pre-submission enquiry remains advisable before paying or completing a full submission.

**Suggested article positioning:** Original Research; computational oral microbiology; aggregate exploratory/hypothesis-generating study. Do not select a mechanism, biomarker, clinical prediction, or AD category.

**为什么可考虑：** 官方范围覆盖口腔疾病、分子遗传学、病理生理学、流行病学和口腔微生物学，并标示为 Web of Science SCIE 收录。

**为什么风险仍高：** 当前包已有外部报告的12条序列字符串和AChE对接汇总，但缺少其与主要来源的逐行链路、样本映射、预测器逐行分数、原始筛选代码、原始对接产物、独立验证和有效受试者层面比较。建议先发预投稿询问信，再决定是否正式投稿和支付费用。

### Secondary target: Scientific Reports

**Fit:** Broad original research across natural sciences and medicine, including computational work.

**Risk:** Scientific robustness and reproducibility remain central. Missing screening lineage, raw docking artefacts and original code are likely more serious than limited novelty. Direct submission is not recommended without an editorial enquiry.

**适配：** 覆盖自然科学和医学原创研究，也可接收计算研究。

**风险：** 该刊仍强调科学稳健性与可重复性。缺少筛选链路、原始对接产物和原始代码比创新有限更可能构成实质问题；不建议未经编辑询问直接投稿。

### Higher-evidence target, not realistic under current constraints: Journal of Oral Microbiology

**Fit:** Official scope includes oral microbiome/genomics, molecular microbiology of systemic disease, ecology, epidemiology, methods and brief reports; external records indicate SCIE indexing.

**Risk:** Topic fit is strong but evidence expectations are higher. The twelve strings are not linked to sample/taxon-resolved discovery, and no functional result is available.

**适配：** 官方范围包含口腔微生物组/基因组学、系统性疾病分子微生物学、生态学、流行病学、方法和简报；外部索引记录显示为 SCIE。

**风险：** 主题适配度高，但证据要求更高；12条字符串尚未连接到样本/分类分辨的发现记录，也没有功能结果。

## Excluded as primary targets / 不作为首选

- **Frontiers in Oral Health:** scope is relevant, but the official 2026 page identifies Web of Science **ESCI**, not SCIE, and explicitly requires appropriate validation for primarily computational public-data studies.
- **BMC Bioinformatics / computational-method journals:** no new algorithm, benchmark, software, or independently reproducible model is supplied.
- **Mechanistic neurodegeneration journals:** no brain exposure, toxicity, target, animal, clinical, or AD endpoint was measured.
- **High-impact microbiome journals:** current sequence/data/code and validation deficits are incompatible with the evidence standard.

## Submission decision gate / 投稿决策门

Proceed to a full journal submission only after the accountable authors:

1. choose and recheck a target journal;
2. send a candid pre-submission enquiry describing the screening-lineage and docking-reproducibility limitations;
3. confirm that the editor will consider an original article with source-reported sequences/scores but without recoverable row-level lineage and raw docking artefacts;
4. complete authorship, affiliation, ethics, funding, conflict, data, and AI declarations;
5. accept that a desk rejection remains likely.

只有在责任作者选定并复核期刊、如实披露逐行链路和原始对接产物缺失、确认编辑愿意考虑这类来源报告序列/分数的原创稿、补齐所有作者与声明信息，并接受仍可能直接退稿后，才建议进入完整投稿流程。
