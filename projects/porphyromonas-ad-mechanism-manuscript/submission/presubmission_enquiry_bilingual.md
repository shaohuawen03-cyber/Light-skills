# Pre-submission enquiry / 预投稿询问信

## English version

**Subject:** Pre-submission enquiry — provenance-aware oral-micropeptide screening with an externally reported AChE docking follow-up

Dear Editor,

We are considering submitting an Original Research Article entitled “Provenance-Aware Multi-Model Prioritization of Periodontitis-Cohort Oral Micropeptides: Aggregate Screening and an Acetylcholinesterase Docking Follow-up.”

The manuscript reconstructs an aggregate oral-smORF screening funnel and integrates a separately authored report of twelve 7–9-aa candidate sequences and AutoDock Vina scores against human acetylcholinesterase PDB 4EY6. It independently recalculates all aggregate percentages, sequence composition and the reported score ordering. It deliberately avoids peptide-level healthy–periodontitis inferential testing because candidate sequences are not independent biological replicates.

We wish to disclose two material limitations before submission. First, the principal source contains counts, thresholds and workflow descriptions but not candidate-level identifiers, participant/sample mappings, row-level predictor scores or original executable code. The twelve externally reported sequences therefore cannot be linked row by row to the principal screening funnel or to the stricter 8-of-12 subset. Second, the external project provides docking means±SD and a summary figure but no prepared receptor/ligand files, exact configuration, seeds, raw runs, logs or poses. The docking was not independently reproduced and is labelled “source-reported” throughout; no binding affinity, residue-contact, molecular-dynamics or disease-mechanism result is claimed.

The expanded manuscript includes 53 curated references, explicit evidence tiers, a reproducibility audit, source-labelled figures and a staged validation roadmap. Its contribution is a transparent, sequence-specific hypothesis package rather than validated target binding or an Alzheimer’s disease mechanism.

Would the journal consider this provenance-aware, hypothesis-generating computational Original Research Article under these conditions, or would candidate-level screening lineage and fully reproducible docking artefacts be mandatory for editorial consideration?

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. We would appreciate guidance on article type and scope fit before formal submission.

## 中文核对版

**主题：** 预投稿询问——保留来源边界的口腔微肽筛选与外部报告AChE对接随访

尊敬的编辑：

我们拟投稿原创研究论文“基于来源边界的牙周炎队列口腔微肽多模型优选：汇总筛选与乙酰胆碱酯酶对接随访”。

稿件重建一条口腔smORF汇总筛选漏斗，并整合另一份作者记录所报告的12条7–9 aa候选序列及其针对人乙酰胆碱酯酶PDB 4EY6的AutoDock Vina分数。研究独立重算所有汇总比例、序列组成和报告分数排序。由于候选序列不是独立生物学重复，稿件明确不实施肽层面的健康—牙周炎推断检验。

投稿前，我们希望如实披露两项实质局限。第一，主要来源仅含计数、阈值和流程描述，不含候选层面标识、参与者/样本映射、逐行预测器分数或原始可执行代码。因此，外部报告的12条序列无法逐行连接至主要筛选漏斗或更严格的8/12子集。第二，外部项目提供对接均值±SD和汇总图，但不含制备后的受体/配体文件、精确配置、种子、原始运行、日志或构象。对接未被独立复现，并在全文标为“来源报告”；稿件不声称结合亲和力、残基接触、分子动力学结果或疾病机制。

扩展稿包含53条整理文献、明确证据层级、可重复性审计、来源标记图件和分阶段验证路线。其贡献是一套透明、序列特异的假设包，而不是经验证的靶标结合或阿尔茨海默病机制。

请问贵刊是否会在上述条件下考虑这类保留来源边界、用于假设生成的计算原创研究？候选层面筛选链路和完整可复现的对接产物是否属于编辑审查的必备条件？

本稿未在其他期刊审理。恳请在正式投稿前指导文章类型与范围适配性。
