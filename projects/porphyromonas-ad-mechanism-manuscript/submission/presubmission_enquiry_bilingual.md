# Pre-submission enquiry / 预投稿询问信

## English version

**Subject:** Pre-submission enquiry — aggregate-level computational prioritization of periodontitis-cohort oral micropeptides

Dear Editor,

We are considering submitting an Original Research Article entitled “Evidence-Bounded Multi-Model Prioritization of Periodontitis-Cohort Oral Micropeptides with Predicted Blood–Brain Barrier Penetration, Neurotoxicity, and Metal-Interaction Features: An Aggregate-Level In Silico Study.”

The manuscript reconstructs and audits a source-reported computational funnel from oral smORFs to a 12-candidate main count and an 8-candidate stricter count. It explicitly treats healthy and periodontitis values as descriptive aggregates, not independent biological replicates, and makes no claim of disease specificity, experimental activity, or Alzheimer’s disease causality.

Before submitting, we wish to disclose a material limitation. The available source package contains aggregate counts, thresholds, and workflow descriptions but not candidate sequences, subject/sample mappings, row-level model scores, or the original executable analysis code. Consequently, the primary pipeline and final candidate identities cannot be independently reproduced from the submission package. Repository code reproduces only aggregate arithmetic, figures, and document assembly. These limitations are stated in the title framing, Abstract, Methods, Discussion, and Data/Code Availability sections.

Would the journal consider an aggregate-level, hypothesis-generating computational prioritization article under these conditions, or would candidate-level data and original code be mandatory for editorial consideration?

We have not submitted the manuscript elsewhere. We would appreciate guidance on article type and scope fit before a formal submission.

## 中文核对版

**主题：** 预投稿询问——牙周炎队列口腔微肽的聚合层面计算优先排序

尊敬的编辑：

我们拟投稿一篇题为“牙周炎队列口腔微肽的证据约束型多模型优先排序：预测血脑屏障穿透、神经毒性与金属相互作用特征的聚合层面计算研究”的原创研究论文。

稿件重建并审计了一条来源报告的计算漏斗，从口腔 smORF 缩减至 12 条主候选计数和 8 条严格候选计数。稿件明确把健康和牙周炎数值视为描述性聚合值，而不是独立生物学重复；不主张疾病特异性、实验活性或阿尔茨海默病因果关系。

在正式投稿前，我们希望如实披露一项实质局限：当前来源包含聚合计数、阈值和流程描述，但不含候选序列、受试者/样本映射、逐行模型分数或原始可执行分析代码。因此，无法仅根据投稿包独立复现主分析流程和最终候选身份。仓库代码只能复现聚合算术、图形和文档组装。稿件在标题定位、摘要、方法、讨论及数据/代码可用性部分均明确陈述这些局限。

请问在上述条件下，贵刊是否会考虑聚合层面、用于假设生成的计算优先排序原创论文？还是候选层面数据和原始代码属于编辑审查的必备条件？

本稿尚未投往其他期刊。希望在正式投稿前获得有关文章类型和范围适配的指导。
