# v3.8.0 manuscript quality summary

## User-directed structural revision

All full and concise English/Chinese manuscripts were revised together:

- the abstract is one continuous, unlabelled paragraph;
- the former abstract Conclusions component was removed rather than merged into
  the paragraph;
- the Statistical analysis subsection was removed;
- the standalone Conclusion section was removed;
- Discussion now proceeds directly to References;
- ordinary main-text paragraphs use a 480-twip (24-point) first-line indent;
- abstract text, headings, keywords, captions, tables, and references remain
  unindented.

The resulting body sizes remain substantive: approximately 5,900 words in full
English and 3,000 words in concise English, with synchronized full and concise
Chinese versions.

## Preserved requirements

The four DOCX deliverables remain titleless, neutral, figure-free, and separate
by language and length. They retain 12-point Times New Roman body text, double
spacing, one-inch margins, black unnumbered headings, editable three-line tables,
and numbered references ordered by first citation. Full variants retain four
tables and 55 references; concise variants retain two tables and 22 references.

The MD analysis remains explicitly ongoing in Materials and methods and
Discussion. The revision neither invents trajectory measurements nor reframes
their temporary absence as a permanent reporting decision.

## Automated acceptance gates

The authoritative audit now requires:

- exactly six top-level sections in order: Abstract, Introduction, Materials and
  methods, Results, Discussion, References;
- one unstructured abstract paragraph with no Conclusions component;
- absence of Statistical analysis and standalone Conclusion headings;
- Discussion-to-References adjacency;
- 480-twip main-text first-line indentation in DOCX;
- one unindented DOCX abstract paragraph;
- figure-free packages and three-line tables;
- synchronized citation inventories and exact first-citation reference order;
- deterministic, byte-identical DOCX rebuilding.

Zotero-live acceptance remains a separate desktop Word/Better BibTeX gate. The
committed DOCX files contain static numbered citation text and do not claim live
Zotero fields.
