# Journal-format alignment for v3.9.0

## Current manuscript presentation

The English manuscripts retain the core original-research sequence of
Introduction, Materials and methods, Results, Discussion, and References. In
response to the user's current formatting instructions, both full and concise
variants now use:

- one unlabelled abstract paragraph;
- no Conclusions component in the abstract;
- no standalone Statistical analysis subsection;
- no standalone Conclusion section;
- Discussion followed directly by References;
- unnumbered section and subsection headings;
- 12-point Times New Roman body text;
- double spacing and one-inch margins;
- a 480-twip (24-point) first-line indent for ordinary main-text paragraphs;
- no first-line indent for the abstract, headings, keywords, captions, tables, or references;
- numbered references in first-citation order;
- editable three-line tables and no embedded figures.
- six full-version tables and three concise-version tables, including complete
  long- and short-branch multidimensional UniDL4BioPep summaries.

The full and concise English abstracts remain below 250 words. The Chinese
manuscripts preserve the same section logic and use an approximately
two-character first-line indent in main-text paragraphs.

## Relationship to the working journal target

The broader typography and research-article organization remain informed by the
Journal of Alzheimer's Disease Reports author instructions:
https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/alr

The single-paragraph abstract and removal of the abstract Conclusions component,
Statistical analysis subsection, and standalone Conclusion section are explicit
user-directed deviations from the previously adopted structured-abstract model.
The standing user requirements for a titleless first page, no page numbers, no
article-body declarations, and neutral filenames also remain in force.

A future target-journal submission may require a visible title page, author and
affiliation information, declarations, pagination, or a different abstract
structure. These missing metadata have not been invented.

## Scientific status

The structural removals do not change the scientific evidence. The manuscript
continues to state in Materials and methods and Discussion that the prespecified
100-ns GROMACS trajectory analysis is ongoing and that stability, convergence,
and contact measurements will be incorporated after analysis and quality
control. No interim MD result has been fabricated.

The Discussion also states the newly explicit length-attrition limitation: all
72 metaproteome-supported, dereplicated long BBB-high candidates were absent
from the 923-member NTxPred2-positive set, whose sequences were all ≤30 aa.
Consequently, the downstream metal/CHEL/FRS cascade and final aggregate set of
12 contain short peptides only. This is reported as a serial-pipeline and
model-applicability limitation, not as evidence that long peptides lack
biological activity.
