# v3.7.0 manuscript quality summary

## Revision focus

- Corrected MD wording: trajectory analysis is ongoing and the prespecified
  stability, convergence, and contact measurements will be incorporated after
  analysis and quality control. Article bodies no longer state that MD results
  will not be reported.
- Expanded the concise English body from approximately 1,825 to 3,182 words
  (about 74%), with synchronized Chinese scientific content.
- Aligned the core manuscript layout with conventional publishable original
  research formatting: structured abstract, unnumbered IMRaD headings,
  12-point Times New Roman, double spacing, one-inch margins, black headings,
  and citation-ordered numbered references.
- Retained the user-required titleless abstract-first format, no page numbers,
  no administrative declarations, figure-free DOCX packages, three-line tables,
  and Conclusion followed directly by References.

## Scientific boundaries

The revised wording does not invent MD measurements. The Methods define the
ongoing 100-ns GROMACS extension and its prespecified outputs; trajectory-derived
measurements remain to be inserted after the analysis is complete. Current
screening and docking limitations, participant-level statistical boundaries,
and the absence of biological validation remain explicit.

## Automated gates

The current audit requires all four variants to pass:

- structured abstract labels and a ≤250-word English abstract;
- unnumbered journal-style section hierarchy;
- minimum substantive body length (full English ≥6,000 words; concise English
  ≥3,000 words; synchronized Chinese character thresholds);
- ongoing-MD and future-integration wording, with prohibited “no MD result”
  formulations absent;
- 12-point, double-spaced, one-inch-margin DOCX styles;
- no figures or media relationships;
- three-line OpenXML table borders;
- first-citation reference order and BibTeX parity;
- deterministic, byte-identical DOCX rebuilds.

Zotero-live acceptance remains a separate desktop Word/Better BibTeX gate as
recorded in `zotero_live_status.json` and
`../references/ZOTERO_WORD_ACCEPTANCE.md`.
