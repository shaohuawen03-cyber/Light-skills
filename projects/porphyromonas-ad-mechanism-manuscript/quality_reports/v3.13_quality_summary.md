# v3.13.0 quality summary

## Release scope

v3.13.0 expands the standalone docking and molecular dynamics (MD) report package to comprehensive deliverables starting from molecular docking of 12 candidate micropeptides and incorporating multi-system 100-ns all-atom GROMACS MD simulations:

- `manuscript/md_alllhrc/full/English.docx` and `English.md`
- `manuscript/md_alllhrc/full/Chinese.docx` and `Chinese.md`

No concise or intermediate MD variant is delivered. The six oral-smORF screening DOCX files under `manuscript/full/`, `manuscript/intermediate/` and `manuscript/concise/` remain byte-for-byte unchanged. No docking or MD result is imported into any screening manuscript.

## Scientific and structural revision

Both standalone reports contain exactly three top-level sections:

1. Analysis methods / 分析方法
2. Results / 结果
3. Discussion / 讨论

Key scientific content implemented in this round:
- **Molecular Docking**: Exhaustive AutoDock Vina docking of all 12 candidate micropeptides (7–9 aa) against human recombinant AChE (PDB 4EY6), reporting scores (-8.25 to -9.60 kcal/mol), intermolecular hydrogen bonds (3–10 bonds), average bond lengths (2.83–3.28 Å), and key interacting residues.
- **PAS Engagement Analysis**: Systematically answers whether candidate micropeptides dock to the Peripheral Anionic Site (PAS). Categorizes peptides into canonical PAS binders (FLLHTTR, YLSLLQR, FCLHLQLR, HVLLLRQCA, HLLTLKKHV, LLHPLRL) and catalytic/gorge neck binders (ALLLHRC, YHHLLCRR). Highlighting:
  - FLLHTTR: Extensive PAS docking (Asp74, Tyr72, His287, Leu289, Phe295), top docking score (-9.60 kcal/mol);
  - LLHPLRL: Dual-site spanning mode bridging core PAS (Trp286, Tyr341) to the catalytic triad (His447) with 10 hydrogen bonds;
  - YLSLLQR: Bridges PAS (Tyr72, Thr75) with catalytic/anionic machinery (-9.49 kcal/mol).
- **Multi-System 100-ns GROMACS MD**: Complete comparative analysis across unliganded apo AChE (single-chain control) and three pathogenic complexes (ALLLHRC, FLLHTTR, YLSLLQR). Confirms receptor tertiary fold preservation (RMSD < 0.22 nm, Rg 2.29–2.32 nm, ΔRg < 0.02 nm, DSSP α-helix ~33–34% and β-sheet ~17%), localized PAS loop flexibility (RMSF 0.080–0.090 nm), persistent intermolecular hydrogen bonds (2.2–4.2 bonds), 7 persistent interface contact pairs, and strong spatial localization (RDF peak g(r) = 43–213).
- **Figures Cited in Text**: Full in-text citations and figure legends for:
  - Figure 1 / 图1: Docking scores and PAS engagement summary (`fig5_docking_scores.{png,svg,pdf}`);
  - Figure 2 / 图2: Apo AChE vs AChE–ALLLHRC 100-ns 6-panel comparison (`fig_compare_ache_vs_alllhrc.{png,svg,pdf}`);
  - Figure 3 / 图3: Apo AChE vs AChE–FLLHTTR 100-ns 6-panel comparison (`fig_compare_ache_vs_fllhttr.{png,svg,pdf}`);
  - Figure 4 / 图4: Apo AChE vs AChE–YLSLLQR 100-ns 6-panel comparison (`fig_compare_ache_vs_ylsllqr.{png,svg,pdf}`).
- **Citation Structure**: Methods and Results contain no in-text citation markup. The Discussion section cites high-impact SCI literature directly (Atanasova et al., Dominy et al., Silman & Sussman, Inestrosa et al., Hampel et al., Bartus et al., Selkoe & Hardy, Dinamarca et al., Poole et al., Cheung et al., Rees & Brimijoin) to explain the mechanistic link between periodontal micropeptides, BBB permeation, PAS active gorge blockade (cholinergic dysfunction), and AChE-mediated amyloid co-nucleation in AD.
- **Three-Line Tables**: Retains three editable three-line tables with 12, 13, and 6 data rows.
