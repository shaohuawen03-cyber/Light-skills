# v3.11.0 quality summary

New deliverables:

- `manuscript/intermediate/English.docx`
- `manuscript/intermediate/Chinese.docx`

## Scientific completeness

The intermediate manuscripts retain:

- all initial-library, filtered-library and branch-specific denominators;
- the complete 22-category long/short UniDL4BioPep multidimensional summary at the supplied ≥0.80 threshold;
- the 3,518 BBB-high total, 3,299 NTxPred2-evaluated candidates, 923 model-positive candidates and the downstream 111, 15, 12 and 8 counts;
- all twelve explicit 7–9-aa sequences, recalculated composition and AChE Vina score ordering;
- the prospective comparative 100-ns GROMACS protocol without inserting the separately reported ALLLHRC result;
- the final limitation that all 72 metaproteome-supported, dereplicated long BBB-high candidates were absent from the all-≤30-aa NTxPred2-positive set, so the downstream metal/CHEL/FRS cascade and final aggregate 12 contained only short peptides;
- the explicit statement that this attrition is a serial model/pipeline limitation and does not prove biological inactivity of long peptides.

The intermediate version reduces the full manuscript’s literature detail and validation discussion while retaining more evidence context, methods, composition data and references than the concise version.

## Formatting and audit results

`quality_reports/intermediate_package_audit.json` returns `PASS` for both language versions. It verifies:

- a titleless, one-paragraph abstract;
- Introduction, Materials and methods, Results, Discussion and References in order;
- no Statistical analysis subsection or standalone Conclusion;
- first-line-indented ordinary body paragraphs;
- 12-point journal body text, double spacing and one-inch margins;
- four editable three-line tables in each DOCX;
- no figure, drawing, header, footer, page number or comment;
- 40 synchronized references in first-citation order;
- complete multidimensional and long-peptide-attrition results;
- the required full > intermediate > concise length relationship;
- unchanged hashes for all pre-existing full, concise and MD DOCX files.

`quality_reports/full_docx_reproducibility.json` additionally requires isolated byte-identical reconstruction of all six screening DOCX files. Citation sources remain BibTeX-linked Markdown with static numbered DOCX text; the files are not represented as Zotero-live fields.
