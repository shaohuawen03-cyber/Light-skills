- `manuscript/md_alllhrc/full/{English,Chinese}.{md,docx}`

## Scientific and structural revision

All six standalone reports contain exactly three top-level sections:

1. Analysis methods / 分析方法
2. Results / 结果
3. Discussion / 讨论

All three lengths embed the same seven PNG figures and retain the same principal numerics (best-run and three-run Vina scores; last-20-ns RMSD, RMSF, SASA, Rg, DSSP helix/sheet, and intermolecular hydrogen bonds). Length differences are narrative only:

- concise: two-paragraph methods, 6-row Table 2 aligned to Figure 4–6 panels A–F, 4-row evidence table, 7 references;
- intermediate: docking + MD methods with four-stage equilibration, 8-row Table 2, 5-row evidence table, 8 references;
- full: complete protocol, 13-row Table 2, 7-row evidence table, 12 references.

Figure concordance (recorded in `manuscript/md_alllhrc/VERSIONS.md`):

- Figure 1 matches `source_materials/md_results/local_vina_docking_summary.csv` and `fig5_docking_scores.png`;
- Figures 2, 3 and S1 are the user-uploaded pose composites A–F / G–L / combined;
- Figures 4–6 are byte-identical to `asd` `gromacs_md/compare_ache_vs_{alllhrc,fllhttr,ylsllqr}/fig_compare.png` and match `compare_summary_*.csv`.
