# v3.12.0 quality summary

## Release scope

v3.12.0 reduces the standalone ALLLHRC–AChE molecular-dynamics package to two separate full reports:

- `manuscript/md_alllhrc/full/English.docx`
- `manuscript/md_alllhrc/full/Chinese.docx`

Each DOCX has a matching Markdown source. The former `manuscript/md_alllhrc/concise/` directory and all four concise MD files were intentionally deleted. No intermediate MD version exists.

The six oral-smORF screening DOCX files under `manuscript/full/`, `manuscript/intermediate/` and `manuscript/concise/` remain byte-for-byte unchanged. No ALLLHRC MD result was added to any screening manuscript.

## Scientific and structural revision

Both standalone MD reports now contain exactly three top-level sections:

1. Analysis methods / 分析方法
2. Results / 结果
3. Discussion / 讨论

The reports contain no displayed title, abstract, keywords, Introduction, citation marker, reference list, Statistical analysis section or standalone Conclusion. They retain:

- the configured 100-ns GROMACS simulation and analysis details;
- all principal AChE, ALLLHRC, complex RMSD, RMSF, center-of-mass RDF, SASA, secondary-structure and hydrogen-bond observations;
- two editable three-line result tables with 9 and 6 data rows;
- explicit disclosure that RMSD summaries derive from a digitized trace;
- explicit disclosure of the inherited AChE–Aβ plot-title discrepancy;
- the Atanasova comparison framework without importing Aβ-specific data;
- limitations arising from one trajectory, absent raw coordinate archive, absent independently seeded replicas and unmeasured biochemical or disease outcomes.

The evidence remains computation-only and descriptive. It does not establish binding affinity, PAS residence, AChE inhibition, altered amyloid assembly, BBB transport, toxicity or an AD mechanism.
