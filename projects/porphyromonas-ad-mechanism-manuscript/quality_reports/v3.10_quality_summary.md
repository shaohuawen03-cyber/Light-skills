## Scientific controls

The manuscripts report the following evidence-controlled observations:

- AChE RMSD diagnostic: 0.1803 ± 0.0220 nm; maximum 0.2320 nm.
- ALLLHRC self-fitted RMSD plateaus: 0.0582 ± 0.0091 nm at 0.0–22.6 ns; 0.1432 ± 0.0161 nm at 23.4–55.6 ns; 0.2694 ± 0.0148 nm at 57.0–100.0 ns.
- Preferred peptide–AChE center-of-mass separation ranges near 1.2 and 1.4 nm.
- Approximate complex and peptide SASA ranges of 210–223 and 10–12 nm².
- Stable plotted secondary-structure fractions, without assigning the unspecified selection to ALLLHRC.
- Intermolecular hydrogen-bond counts of 0–11, generally lower late in the run.

Atanasova et al. (2020), doi:10.2478/cait-2020-0068, is used as an analytical framework only. Its Aβ contact residues, AChE 344–361 residence region, PAS behavior and 1-μs conclusions are not transferred to ALLLHRC.

The manuscripts disclose that the RMSD diagnostic was calculated from a digitized trace and that the supplied plot retains an inherited AChE–Aβ title despite the ALLLHRC directory and user designation. A topology/trajectory manifest is required to close the identity-provenance discrepancy. Complete raw trajectories and independent replicas are unavailable, so no affinity, PAS residence, inhibition, amyloid, BBB, toxicity or disease claim is made.
