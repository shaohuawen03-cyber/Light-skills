# v3.9.0 manuscript quality summary

## Restored principal-source results

All full and concise English/Chinese manuscripts now retain the principal
source's complete multidimensional UniDL4BioPep summaries:

- long branch: 22 model outputs against 953 healthy-labelled and 1,032
  periodontitis-labelled candidates;
- short branch: 22 model outputs against 30,557 and 32,754 candidates;
- a common high-confidence threshold of model output ≥0.80;
- exact source counts and percentages, including the long-branch Bitter,
  Anti-parasitic, Antimicrobial, BBB, NeuroPred, and Toxicity outputs and the
  short-branch near-saturated Antimicrobial_activity output.

The full manuscripts use separate long- and short-branch tables. The concise
manuscripts retain all 22 functional categories in one compact paired table.
Every value remains descriptive: functional outputs can overlap, candidate
sequences are not independent participant-level observations, and parallel
percentages are not interpreted as disease enrichment.

## Explicit long-peptide attrition limitation

The Results and final Discussion limitations now state the complete available
sequence of aggregate facts:

1. the periodontitis-labelled, metaproteome-supported and dereplicated long
   branch contained 1,032 candidates;
2. 72 of those 31–50-aa candidates were BBB-high;
3. all 923 NTxPred2-positive candidates were reported to be ≤30 aa;
4. no long peptide therefore remained in the neurotoxicity-positive set feeding
   the downstream mebipred metal-binding and AnOxPePred CHEL/FRS filters;
5. the final aggregate set of 12 consequently contains short peptides only.

The manuscripts explicitly avoid treating this attrition as proof that long
peptides lack neurotoxicity or metal-related activity. Serial thresholds, model
applicability/calibration, and web-predictor implementation remain alternative
explanations that cannot be separated without candidate-level outputs.

## Preserved formatting and scientific boundaries

The four DOCX files remain separate, titleless, figure-free manuscripts with a
single-paragraph abstract, no abstract Conclusions component, no standalone
Statistical analysis or Conclusion section, 12-point Times New Roman body text,
double spacing, one-inch margins, 480-twip main-text first-line indentation,
three-line tables, and first-citation-ordered references. The full variants now
contain six tables each; the concise variants contain three tables each.

The MD analysis remains ongoing, and no trajectory measurement has been
invented. Zotero-live acceptance remains a separate desktop Word/Better BibTeX
gate; committed DOCX files contain static numbered citations only.
