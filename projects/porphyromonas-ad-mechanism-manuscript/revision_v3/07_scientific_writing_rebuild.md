# Revision v3 — Stage 7: scientific writing rebuild / 科学写作重建

Date: 2026-08-12

## Expansion outcome

The English manuscript was rebuilt as a full scientific-content draft of more than 7,000 whitespace-delimited words, with a parallel Chinese version and shared bibliography. It now includes:

- structured abstract with exact denominators and provenance boundaries;
- expanded smORF, oral-omics, predictor, AChE/PAS, metal and epidemiology rationale;
- explicit three-tier evidence model;
- separate methods for principal screening, external sequences and external docking;
- four main result tables plus three figures;
- detailed discussion of model domain, pseudoreplication, docking limitations, causal restraint and experimental stopping rules;
- complete declarations with placeholders rather than invented author metadata.

## Language architecture

Terms are standardized across languages: “periodontitis-cohort branch,” “BBB-high/model-positive,” “source-reported,” “not independently reproduced,” and “hypothesis-generating.” “Binding affinity,” “validated mechanism,” “disease-specific,” and similar overclaims are not used for the current candidates.

## Reference integration

The shared corpus increased from 25 to 53 curated DOI-bearing records. Citations are used in the body rather than appended as decorative bibliography.

## Readiness boundary

The expanded manuscript is suitable for accountable-author review and candid editorial enquiry, not represented as guaranteed acceptable or fully reproducible.
