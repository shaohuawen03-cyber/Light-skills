# Revision v3 — Stage 1: topic and claim architecture / 选题与主张架构

Date: 2026-08-12

## Decision

Retain the user-selected **aggregate-only Option D**, but use the user-designated external v0.4 record to make the endpoint sequence-specific where defensible. Reframe the study as a provenance-aware computational reconstruction with an AChE docking follow-up, not as a validated periodontitis-to-AD mechanism.

## Central question

What can be concluded from (i) an arithmetically auditable aggregate screening funnel and (ii) an external report of twelve sequence identities and AChE Vina summaries when their row-level screening lineage and raw docking artefacts are absent?

## Falsifiable near-term questions

- Do the twelve strings satisfy the reported length and composition statements?
- Do the reported means/SDs have the stated ranges and ordering?
- Can each string be linked to a principal-source screening row and the stricter 8-of-12 subset?
- Can the docking be reproduced from deposited inputs and logs?

The first two are answered by deterministic audit; the latter two remain negative because the required artefacts are absent.

## Claim ceiling

Permitted: aggregate retention, predictor-positive counts, twelve source-reported sequences, recomputed composition, source-reported docking-score ordering, limitations, and validation roadmap.

Prohibited: periodontitis specificity, current-cohort expression, organism attribution, measured BBB crossing, toxicity, verified metal binding, reproduced docking, residue contacts, binding free energy, completed MD, AChE modulation, AD causality, or a validated mechanism.

## Output

The title, abstract, Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, figures, and submission documents were rebuilt around this claim ceiling.
