# Revision v3 — Stage 3: evidence synthesis / 证据综合

Date: 2026-08-12

## Three-tier evidence model

- **Tier A — principal source:** aggregate counts, thresholds and workflow narrative. This tier alone defines the screening funnel.
- **Tier B — user-designated external v0.4:** twelve sequence strings and Vina mean±SD summaries. Classified as cross-repository author-supplied summaries, not independently reproduced results.
- **Tier C — peer-reviewed literature:** biological rationale, counterevidence and validation standards. It does not fill missing results.

## Integrated synthesis

The literature supports the legitimacy of searching microbiome smORFs but requires translation/proteomic and functional validation. Oral omics studies demonstrate why exact matches from heterogeneous resources cannot be relabelled current-cohort expression or disease specificity. Predictor papers justify model identity but not calibration in this sequence domain. AChE/PAS and metal/tau literature motivates target and assay questions while preserving the gap between analogy and proof. Periodontitis–AD observational evidence is balanced with Mendelian-randomization counterevidence.

## External-v0.4 acceptance

Accepted: twelve printed sequences; deterministic composition; twelve printed Vina means/SDs; the statement that the external report used AChE PDB 4EY6, Vina 1.2.5, and a 40×40×40 Å³ PAS-centred box.

Rejected as established results: pose contacts, SD interpretation, affinity, selectivity, AChE function, Aβ modulation, MD outcomes and disease mechanism.
