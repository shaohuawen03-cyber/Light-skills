# Revision v3 — Stage 2: literature search and novelty / 文献检索与创新性

Date: 2026-08-12

## Corpus expansion

The prior 25-reference set and the external v0.4 bibliography were curated record by record into a 53-reference corpus. The revised set now covers:

1. smORF discovery, annotation, translation and multi-omics detection;
2. oral metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, HOMD/eHOMD, and metaproteomics;
3. UniDL4BioPep, BBB predictors, NTxPred2, mebipred and AnOxPePred;
4. AD overview, AChE/PAS structural biology and AChE–Aβ interactions;
5. metal dyshomeostasis, Cu–tau/Aβ chemistry and short neuroactive peptides;
6. periodontitis–AD association, Mendelian-randomization counterevidence, and organism-specific virulence context;
7. AutoDock Vina and flexible peptide-docking standards.

## Curation rules

No wholesale import was permitted. Duplicate records, an erroneous correction-note identifier, material traceable only to excluded files, and records unused by the revised argument were omitted. DOI-set parity is required across English, Chinese, verification record and BibTeX.

## Novelty assessment

The work does not introduce a new predictor, omics dataset, docking algorithm, or validated mechanism. Its defensible novelty is a bilingual, provenance-aware reconstruction that converts anonymous aggregate endpoints into a sequence-specific hypothesis set while exposing the unresolved principal-source lineage and docking reproducibility gap.

## Risk

Literature depth cannot compensate for missing candidate rows, raw docking artefacts, sample-level statistics or experiments. The reference corpus is context and interpretation control, not substitute evidence.
